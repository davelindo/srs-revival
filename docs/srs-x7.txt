Sony SRS-X7 custom firmware

The X7 can run Linux on its network module and play local audio through its
built-in speakers. Sony’s controller retains the factory tuning. The local
dashboard displays volume and EQ. Two system slots and wired rescue provide
a way back if a new system fails to start.

AirPlay 2 reception works over Ethernet through the digital audio output.
Audible AirPlay playback, Wi-Fi streaming, multiroom playback and Spotify
Connect playback are unverified. The kit has no Google Cast support.

The source kit includes the X7 software and an offline image packager.
Owners supply their own Sony and Nokia files and SSH public key. Installing
the resulting image on a factory-stock X7 is unverified.

Download and build instructions
https://github.com/davelindo/srs-revival/releases/tag/x7-source-0.1.0
